Design a mobile-first, single-page wedding invitation website for an Indian wedding. Guests will open it from a WhatsApp link on their phones, so design for a 390px-wide phone screen first, with a desktop version that stays centred and narrow.

STYLE
Indian traditional, rich and refined. It should feel like the invitation of an upper-middle-class to affluent family: elegant and understated luxury, never flashy. Use sober, muted colours, such as deep emerald or bottle green, dusty rose, champagne gold, warm beige, or navy with antique gold. Avoid bright, neon or saturated colours. Draw on traditional Indian motifs used with restraint: a jharokha or mehrab arch, paisley, lotus, mandala line-work, marigold or jasmine garlands, and fine gold borders. Use an elegant serif for the names and headings and a clean, readable font for the body text. Leave generous white space so it looks premium.
